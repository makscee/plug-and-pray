# thaw

You are an old machine waking blind inside a dead god's body. At first you have only a map: you click it and walk, and a radar sweeps for heartbeats.

Everything you become is a module you find and install: eyes, radars, legs, lamps, weapons, power cells. The first eye you find turns the screen into a coarse grey view; better eyes sharpen it into colour, and extra eyes layer what they see on top. Modules are rare finds, not loot piles: a find you already own upgrades it (I, II, III), and printers and altars upgrade them for scrap. Slots, power and a small bag make every module a choice; the first eye alone drinks most of your power.

Four floors down to the god's heart, each with its own trick and its own keeper. Fight the first two in melee, kiting swarms. On floor 3 you find saint's reins: take the reins and fight in first person, shooting and dodging bullet patterns. The heart is waiting at the bottom.

- Click the map, the orange words and the buttons, or type commands: every button types one. `help` lists them, `where` tells you where to go.
- In first person: move the mouse or trackpad (or ←/→, Q/E) to look, WASD to walk, hold Space or click to shoot. A trackpad is enough; no mouse needed.
- Die and you wake again on a new world. A run takes 20–30 minutes.
- Desktop browser, sound on. Best at 1280x720 or bigger.

Made solo in 48 hours for the Ludum Dare 60 Compo, theme "Old Tech".
